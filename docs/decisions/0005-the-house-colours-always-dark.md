# 0005 - The house colours, always dark

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

The user asked for the house colour scheme found in their other repositories
(Good Idea's `docs/COLOUR.md`, used by ScaleView, Transient Shuffler and the
rest).

## Decision

The same values: ground `#23272E`, sunken `#1A1D23`, roll `#111419` behind
the drawings, controls `#A9AFBA` (hover `#C0C6CF`, held `#8F96A2`) with ink
`#14171C` on every button, text `#DDE1E7` and `#B0B6C0`, rules `#3A404A`,
the sound level `#6D7581`, focus `#F2F4F7`, yellow `#FFF200` only for what is
chosen (segmented choices, the selected note), red `#D2483F` as a mark with
`#FF9E96` for warning text. No light look.

## Consequences

- *Written* is the control grey and *Heard* the focus white: told apart by
  shape as well (ticks at the top, lines below), not colour alone.
