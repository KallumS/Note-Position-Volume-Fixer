Note Fixer for Mac
==================

Measures your sample libraries: it plays every key of an instrument at
several dynamics, articulation by articulation, and notes down how late and
how loud each one really sounds. REAPER's "Note Fixer - Fix Selected Items"
and the Note Position & Volume Fixer page use what it saves to fix your parts.

Needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on Apple Silicon. Your instruments must be
installed as Audio Units (AU): Kontakt, SINE, Spitfire, Engine and the
Synchron Player all are.

Installing
----------

1. Drag "Note Fixer.app" into your Applications folder.
2. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) it and choose Open, then
   Open again. macOS asks because the app isn't signed with an Apple
   Developer ID; after this it opens normally.

   If macOS says it "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal and run:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Note Fixer.app"

   then open it again. (This removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark
   that macOS puts on files from a browser.)

Using it
--------

1. Add Instrument..., choose the instrument, load the patch in its window,
   and press Remember This Patch.
2. Import Articulations... from a BabylonWaves Art Conductor map for Cubase
   (.expressionmap) or Logic (.plist), any Cubase expression map, Logic
   articulation set or Reaticulate bank (.reabank). Or add them by hand.
3. Choose the keys, levels and takes, and press Measure All.

Profiles go to REAPER's Data/Note Fixer/Profiles folder when REAPER is
installed (so REAPER's Fix Selected Items finds them), otherwise to
~/Library/Application Support/Note Fixer/Profiles. "Show in Finder" opens it.
